In racing terms, drivers at the back of the pack, behind the race leaders, experience something called *bad air*. This is when your car is running through hot air that has been through the exhausts of the cars ahead. This air slows you down, because it does not provide the necessary cooling effect that the engine expects.

I think something similar happens to those of us in creative fields, who are not at some forefront of exploration. When your work involves mature and developed ideas, passed down from other people, your creative engine slows down. It is not required to do any significant problem solving.

The answer, is also simple. Move to that unclogged road where you see no cars ahead of you. It is easier to do here than in an F1 race, because instead of a single track, you can diverge almost anywhere and find new ground.
